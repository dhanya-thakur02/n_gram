N-grams are runs of n words in a row

Unigram (n = 1)

A single word.

natural
Bigram (n = 2)

Two neighbouring words, in their original order.

natural language
Trigram (n = 3)

Three neighbouring words, in their original order.

natural language processing

The words must be next to each other and keep their order. Examples here are lowercase and have no punctuation.

FORMULA

How many n-grams does a sentence have?

number of n-grams = T − n + 1

T is the number of words and n is the size of the n-gram. Example: "the cat sat on the mat" has T = 6, so it has 6 − 2 + 1 = 5 bigrams.
